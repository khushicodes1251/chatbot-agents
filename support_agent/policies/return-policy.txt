Harbor & Co. Return Policy

Electronics sold by Harbor & Co. may be returned within 15 days of delivery.
Electronics include headphones, speakers, and cables.

A defective item may be returned even if the original box has been opened.
Items must otherwise be unused and in resaleable condition for a standard return.

To start a return, the customer provides the order id and the reason for return.
Approved returns receive a prepaid return label by email.

Returns requested after 15 days from delivery are not accepted unless the item is covered by warranty.
