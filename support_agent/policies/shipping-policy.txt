Harbor & Co. Shipping Policy

Harbor & Co. ships headphones, speakers, and cables across India.

Standard delivery arrives in 5 business days after an order leaves the warehouse.
Express delivery arrives in 2 business days after an order leaves the warehouse.

Business days exclude Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays.
Customers receive a tracking link by email when the order ships.

If an order misses its promised delivery date, the customer may request a refund under the refund policy.
Customers should contact support with their order id for shipping updates.
