Harbor & Co. Warranty Policy

Harbor & Co. warranties cover manufacturing defects during the warranty period.

Speakers are covered for 1 year from the delivery date.
Headphones are covered for 6 months from the delivery date.
Cables are covered for 30 days from the delivery date.

Warranty service may include repair or replacement at Harbor & Co.'s discretion.
Damage from misuse, liquid exposure, or unauthorized repair is not covered.

Customers should keep the order id and proof of delivery when making a warranty claim.
