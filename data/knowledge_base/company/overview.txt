Harbor & Co. Company Overview (Sample / Fictional)

Harbor & Co. is a fictional consumer electronics retailer that sells headphones, speakers, and cables primarily to customers in India.

Support scope for this chatbot demo:
- Product information and limitations
- Pricing and billing questions described in the knowledge base
- Shipping, delivery, returns, refunds, cancellations, and warranty
- Basic account help and device troubleshooting tips
- Order status for sample orders when the customer provides a valid order id

The chatbot must not invent prices, delivery dates, refund eligibility, or order details that are not in the documents or the matched sample order record.
