Harbor & Co. Device Troubleshooting (Sample / Fictional)

QuietBuds will not connect over Bluetooth:
1. Confirm Bluetooth is enabled on the phone or laptop.
2. Place both earbuds in the case, close the lid for 10 seconds, then reopen and retry pairing.
3. Forget the previous QuietBuds entry in the device Bluetooth list, then pair again.
4. Try another device to see whether the issue follows the earbuds or the phone.
5. If still unpaired after these steps and the product is inside warranty, start a warranty claim with the order id.

SoundBar Mini has no sound:
1. Confirm the source (AUX or Bluetooth) matches the cable or paired device.
2. Raise volume on both the SoundBar Mini and the source device.
3. Try a different cable or re-pair Bluetooth.
4. Power-cycle the speaker (unplug for 30 seconds, then reconnect).
5. If there is still no audio and the unit may be defective, use the return window or warranty process as applicable.

Link USB-C cable does not charge:
1. Try a different USB-C port and a known-good charger.
2. Inspect the cable ends for debris or damage.
3. Test with another compatible device.
4. If the cable fails on multiple devices inside the 30-day cable warranty, start a warranty claim.

Wave speaker battery drains quickly:
1. Lower volume and disable unused Bluetooth connections when idle.
2. Fully charge overnight with the supplied or a compatible cable.
3. If battery life is far below expectations soon after delivery, discuss return eligibility within 15 days or warranty for manufacturing defects.
