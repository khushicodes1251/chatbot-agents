Harbor & Co. Shipping and Delivery Policy (Sample / Fictional)

Harbor & Co. ships headphones, speakers, and cables across India in this sample dataset.

Delivery options after an order leaves the warehouse:
- Standard delivery: arrives in 5 business days after the order leaves the warehouse
- Express delivery: arrives in 2 business days after the order leaves the warehouse

Business days exclude Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays.

Tracking and updates:
- Customers receive a tracking link by email when the order ships.
- Customers should contact support with their order id for shipping updates.

Missed delivery promises:
- If an order misses its promised delivery date, the customer may request a refund under the refund policy.
- Late delivery refund eligibility applies to the item price rules described in the refund documents.

Address changes:
- Address changes are only possible while the order status is processing.
- Once shipped or in transit, support cannot redirect the carrier in this sample policy.
