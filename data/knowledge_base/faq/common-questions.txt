Harbor & Co. Common Customer Questions (Sample / Fictional)

Q: How long does standard shipping take?
A: Standard delivery arrives in 5 business days after the order leaves the warehouse.

Q: How long does express shipping take?
A: Express delivery arrives in 2 business days after the order leaves the warehouse.

Q: How long do refunds take to show on my statement?
A: Allow 5 to 7 business days after the refund is issued.

Q: Can I cancel after shipping?
A: No. Shipped or in-transit orders cannot be cancelled. You may return after delivery if eligible.

Q: What is the return window?
A: 15 days from delivery for eligible electronics, with condition rules in the return policy.

Q: What is the QuietBuds warranty length?
A: 6 months from delivery for manufacturing defects.

Q: What does QuietBuds cost in this sample catalog?
A: 79.99 (fictional sample price).

Q: My payment was declined. Is my order placed?
A: No. Declined payments do not create an order in this sample flow.

Q: I need a human.
A: Human escalation is available for disputes, identity changes, warranty scheduling, and unresolved issues during support hours (see hours-and-escalation).

Different phrasings customers may use:
- "Where is my refund?" → refund timing policy plus order id if they want a specific order checked
- "Stop my package" → cancellation rules for shipped/in_transit
- "Earbuds won't pair" → QuietBuds Bluetooth troubleshooting steps
