Harbor & Co. Payment Failures and Duplicate Charges (Sample / Fictional)

Failed or declined payments:
- If a payment is declined by the bank or card network, the order is not placed and no inventory is reserved in this sample flow.
- Common customer steps: confirm card details, try another payment method, or ask the card issuer why the charge was declined.
- Harbor & Co. support in this demo cannot unlock a customer's bank account or override a bank decline.

Pending authorizations:
- Some banks show a temporary authorization that drops off if the payment does not complete.
- Temporary authorizations are not the same as a completed charge. Completed charges appear on the sample order as a paid order with an order id.

Duplicate charges:
- If a customer reports two identical completed charges for one intended purchase, ask for the order id(s) shown on their receipt or bank statement.
- If two sample order ids exist for the same customer and product on the same day, treat that as a possible duplicate and escalate to human support for a manual review.
- Do not refund a duplicate charge automatically in chat. Follow the refund policy only for eligible returns, late delivery, or damaged arrival, or escalate duplicates.

Refunds of payments:
- Refund timing after an approved return is described in the refund policy (typically 5 to 7 business days to appear on the statement).
- Express shipping upgrade fees are not refunded unless Harbor & Co. caused a delay, per the refund policy.
