Harbor & Co. Warranty and Repairs (Sample / Fictional)

Coverage:
- Harbor & Co. warranties cover manufacturing defects during the warranty period.

Warranty periods from the delivery date:
- Speakers (including SoundBar Mini and Wave): 1 year
- Headphones (QuietBuds): 6 months
- Cables (Link USB-C): 30 days

Service options:
- Warranty service may include repair or replacement at Harbor & Co.'s discretion.
- Damage from misuse, liquid exposure, or unauthorized repair is not covered.

How to claim:
- Customers should keep the order id and proof of delivery when making a warranty claim.
- For claims after the return window but inside warranty, use warranty service instead of a standard return.

Not covered examples:
- Lost charging cases without evidence of a manufacturing defect
- Water damage to non-waterproof products
- Frayed cables caused by bending or chewing
