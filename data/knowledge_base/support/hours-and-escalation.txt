Harbor & Co. Support Hours and Escalation (Sample / Fictional)

Sample support hours:
- Chat and email support: Monday through Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 India Standard Time (IST)
- Closed Saturdays, Sundays, and listed public holidays in this sample policy
- Messages received outside hours are answered on the next business day in this sample process

When to escalate to a human support representative:
- Duplicate payment disputes that need manual finance review
- Identity-sensitive account email or ownership changes
- Warranty claims that need inspection scheduling
- Situations where documents do not contain enough information to resolve the request
- Safety issues or damaged products where the customer reports injury risk

How escalation works in this sample:
- Collect the customer's order id when available, a short description of the issue, and the troubleshooting or policy steps already tried.
- Tell the customer that a human agent will follow up during support hours.
- Do not invent a phone number, WhatsApp number, or physical address. This sample dataset does not publish a public phone line.
- For this local demo, "escalation" means explaining the next step and that human review is required; no real ticket system is connected.
