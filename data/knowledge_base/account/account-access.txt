Harbor & Co. Account Access (Sample / Fictional)

Account basics in this sample store:
- Customers sign in with the email used at checkout.
- Order history and tracking links appear on the account orders page in this sample process.

Password and sign-in help:
- If a customer cannot sign in, advise using the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page to reset access.
- Support agents in this demo cannot see or tell the customer their password.
- After a reset email is requested, the customer should check inbox and spam folders.

Email or account changes:
- Customers can update the account email from account settings after signing in.
- Support should not change an account email in chat without verification steps. This sample dataset requires escalating identity-sensitive account changes to human support.

Security:
- Never ask the customer to share their full password in chat.
- Never ask for full card numbers. Sample orders only store a card_ending value for reference.
