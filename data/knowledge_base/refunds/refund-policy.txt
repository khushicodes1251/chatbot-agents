Harbor & Co. Refund Policy (Sample / Fictional)

When refunds are issued:
- An approved return is refunded to the original payment method.
- Refunds are issued after the returned item is received and inspected.

Automatic eligibility examples:
- An item that arrives damaged is eligible for a refund.
- An order that misses its promised delivery date is eligible for a refund.

Refund amounts:
- Refund amounts match the item price paid on the order.
- Shipping fees for express upgrades are not refunded unless Harbor & Co. caused the delay.

Timing:
- Customers should allow 5 to 7 business days for the refund to appear on their statement after the refund is issued.

Edge cases:
- A cancelled processing order is refunded to the original payment method (see cancellation policy).
- Duplicate-charge disputes are not auto-refunded in chat; escalate for manual review (see payment-failures document).
- Warranty repairs or replacements follow the warranty document and are not the same as a return refund.
