Harbor & Co. Products and Features (Sample / Fictional)

Harbor & Co. currently sells three product lines in this sample catalog: headphones, speakers, and cables.

Harbor QuietBuds Headphones
- Wireless earbuds for everyday listening
- Bluetooth pairing with phones and laptops that support standard Bluetooth audio
- Up to about 6 hours of listening on a single charge in typical use (approximate; battery life varies)
- Charging case included
- Limitation: not rated for swimming or deep-water use; brief sweat and light rain are acceptable
- Limitation: does not include active noise cancellation in this sample product description

Harbor SoundBar Mini
- Compact speaker intended for desks and small rooms
- Wired AUX input and Bluetooth input
- Built-in volume and source buttons
- Limitation: not a full home-theater soundbar; bass is limited in small enclosures
- Limitation: does not include a voice-assistant microphone in this sample description

Harbor Link USB-C Cable
- Short USB-C charging and data cable for compatible devices
- Supports charging and USB data transfer on devices that use USB-C
- Limitation: cable length in this sample catalog is 1 meter
- Limitation: not a video/display alternate-mode cable unless the customer's device and cable packaging both say so

Harbor Wave Portable Speaker
- Compact portable Bluetooth speaker listed in some sample orders
- Battery powered for travel and desks
- Limitation: not waterproof; keep away from liquid spills

If a customer asks about a product not listed here, say the sample catalog does not include that product and offer to help with QuietBuds, SoundBar Mini, Link USB-C, or Wave.
