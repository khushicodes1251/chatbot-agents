Harbor & Co. Pricing and Billing (Sample / Fictional)

Sample catalog prices (INR are not used in this demo; prices below are fictional USD amounts used by the sample orders):

- Harbor QuietBuds Headphones: 79.99
- Harbor SoundBar Mini: 129.99
- Harbor Link USB-C Cable: 14.99
- Harbor Wave Portable Speaker: 89.99

Billing rules in this sample dataset:
- The amount charged matches the product price on the order unless a documented promotion applies.
- This sample dataset does not define live coupon codes. If a customer asks about a promo code that is not written here, say the sample documents do not list that code.
- Customers are billed when the order moves from cart to paid/processing in the sample store flow.
- Itemized receipts are sent to the email on the account when an order is placed (process description only; no real email is sent in this demo).

Taxes and currency:
- Sample order amounts are shown as simple product prices without a separate tax line.
- Do not invent tax rates, GST details, or currency conversions that are not written here.

If a customer disputes a charge amount, compare the claimed amount to the sample order price when they provide an order id. If the amounts differ and documents do not explain the difference, escalate to human support using the escalation document.
