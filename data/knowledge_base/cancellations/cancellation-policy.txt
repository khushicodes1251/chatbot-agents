Harbor & Co. Cancellation Policy (Sample / Fictional)

When cancellation is allowed:
- An order can be cancelled while its status is processing.
- A shipped or in-transit order cannot be cancelled.

How to cancel:
- To cancel a processing order, the customer must provide the order id.
- Cancelled orders are refunded to the original payment method.

If the order already shipped or is in transit:
- Support cannot stop a carrier once the package is in transit.
- The customer may wait for delivery and then request a return if eligible under the return policy.

Edge case — processing vs shipped:
- Status processing: cancellation allowed with order id.
- Status shipped or in_transit: cancellation denied; offer return guidance after delivery if eligible.
